
## What's different in PrusSLASlicer

PrusSLASlicer is PrusaSlicer with support for 3rd-party resin (SLA) printers, starting with the **Phrozen Sonic Mega 8K S**.

- In the Configuration Wizard, go to the **Other SLA** page, tick **Phrozen SLA** (or **Elegoo SLA**), then pick your printer on the page that appears.
- Exports are converted to the printer's own file format (for the Sonic Mega 8K S: `.prz`) by **UVtools**, a free program.
- The first time you export, PrusSLASlicer offers to download UVtools (about 75 MB) and install it for you. Click **Download and install**.

The exposure and lift settings are starting values. Print an exposure test before your first real print.
